# M4 · Detect

**Goal (architecture Day 4):** tactic taxonomy, labeled utterances, classifier vs. LLM zero-shot baseline, full test set, risk score with tactics, evaluation screen. **Demo: metrics table on screen.**

## What was built
```
backend/detector/taxonomy.py   8 tactics: label for Eszter, definition, honest look-alike, "say this" reply
backend/detector/rules.py      high-precision patterns per tactic, with honest-look-alike exceptions
backend/detector/classifier.py all-MiniLM-L6-v2 embeddings + one logistic regression per tactic (model.joblib, 15 KB)
backend/pipeline/tactics.py    pressure detector: each finished caller sentence -> tactic.flagged (strong/mild)
backend/pipeline/suggestions.py  + a "say this" per tactic; risk +15 strong / +3 mild per tactic
eval/data/                     train.jsonl (240) + train_extra.jsonl (130) by Sonnet; test.jsonl (80) by Haiku
backend/fixtures/calls/        12 test calls (5 manipulative, 7 honest incl. 3 hard honest), scripts + labels + audio
eval/train_detector.py         trains the classifier (thresholds for F0.5 on training data only)
eval/run_eval.py               utterance + call level (text) + audio replays -> eval.sqlite, latest.json
frontend: EvalScreen ("How Tenor is measured"), TacticsCard (live)
docs/tactics.md                the taxonomy, generated from code
```

**Honest reading:** Gemini zero-shot is more accurate on this synthetic set. The local detector stays on the live path for latency (~75× faster), no quota (Gemini returned 429s even one line at a time), offline use and cost. Stage latency with 4 calls at once: detector ~20 ms per sentence, partial words ~300 ms, final words ~720 ms.

## How we got here (one disclosed round of feedback)
Run 1: precision **0.60**, and **5 of 7 honest calls rated high**. Thresholds tuned on Sonnet's lines didn't transfer to Haiku's. The causes: short small talk ("Is now a good time?" → urgency 0.87) and same-topic opposites ("feel free to talk it over with your family" → secrecy 0.94). Fixed on principle and on training data only: 130 more training lines of those *kinds*, F0.5 thresholds, and "strong" only with a rule or p ≥ 0.9. The test lines were never changed, but because the changes were prompted by run 1, the numbers are slightly optimistic. The Gemini baseline first looked much worse (recall 0.41) because rate-limited lines counted as "no tactic". After retrying with backoff, all 80 lines were answered.

## What your read-aloud dialogue (Silverline) caught on real audio
- "+80% this year" was checked against Bluewater instead of **Apple**. Fixed: the subject is the company in the claim's own clause; it's now checked against Apple's live data (+26.1% → "Doesn't match data").
- "The next Apple" became the offer ("BWLT (was AAPL)"). Fixed: a ticker is on offer only if introduced or priced.
- An LLM-found "name" overwrote the caller's. Fixed: the LLM can fill gaps but never overwrite, and company names are rejected as names.
- The caller spoke first, so the first-turn rule labeled their intro "You" and the name was lost. Fixed: lines relabeled as the caller's are reprocessed.

## Known issues
- **Within full calls, tactic recall is lower than on single lines** (crypto recovery 0.40, gold 0.50). Their risk is still critical through other signals.
- Five honest calls get a few false *mild* flags. Their risk stays low or medium, as designed.
- The benchmark is synthetic: AI-written lines, TTS-voiced calls with macOS voices. It's not real-world accuracy.
- The live LLM shares the per-minute quota that rate-limited the baseline.
