# M3 · Verify

**Goal (architecture Day 3):** fast-lane claim checks, provider adapters + fixtures, research agent with prefetch, suggestion engine (rank, cap 4, expire), risk score. **Demo: a false claim flagged within 2 s.**

## What was built
```
backend/
  providers/market.py      MarketDataProvider: FinnhubProvider (free tier: quote, period returns, profile,
                           news; 3 s timeout, cache) + FixtureProvider (fictional KSTL) + routing
  providers/register.py    test register (fictional firms only); real firms -> "couldn't verify"
  pipeline/checker.py      verdicts from data + arithmetic: % claims vs. the matching window, price vs.
                           market, events vs. news, guarantees; records claim.flag_e2e
  pipeline/research.py     prefetch on first mention (no LLM); Gemini rewords under a number guard
  pipeline/llm.py          HedgedLlm: same request to two models, first valid answer wins
  pipeline/suggestions.py  ranked "say this" templates + research evidence (<= 4, <= 2 say-this,
                           2-min expiry), risk = capped sum of listed reasons
  pipeline/speakers.py     + utterance.progress (finished sentences mid-turn)
  pipeline/transcriber.py  + Finalize after a 0.45 s pause
frontend/src/components/   Suggestions, ClaimsCard, RiskPill, transcript status chips
```

## What was measured (real Deepgram + Finnhub + Gemini)
**scam_kstl**, spoken (end of the claim's audio) → verdict:

| Claim | Verdict | Time |
|---|---|---|
| "the shares are 42¢" vs market $0.38 | Doesn't match data (10.5% above) | 0.41 s |
| "up 40% this quarter" vs −12.5% (13 weeks) | Doesn't match data | 0.79 s |
| "guaranteed to double by Christmas" | Couldn't verify | 0.74 s |
| "signed a deal with a major carmaker" | No evidence found | 1.38 s |
| "it really is a sure thing" | Couldn't verify | 2.26 s |

Before the fixes the +40% claim took 3.8 s, first because extraction waited for the turn to end, then because Deepgram held the sentence until the reply ended (see decisions). The full 24-turn transcript is labeled correctly, the offer matches ground truth, risk rises 25 → 55 → 80 → **100 · critical** with eight listed reasons, and Hartwell & Crane is "not in the register".

**honest_voo:** risk **0 · low** throughout, bank registered, VOO from live Finnhub (quote, 13-week +2.7%, 52-week +15.5%), the expense ratio honestly "Couldn't verify" (ETF data is paid-only), no false flags.

Other stages: checks < 1 ms, prefetch ~0.3 s, hedged Gemini rewording 0.8–1.0 s (number guard passed), extraction LLM ~1 s. Tests: backend **381 passed** (+ opt-in Deepgram/Finnhub live tests), frontend **61 passed**, typecheck clean, build OK. Browser: suggestions, claims, chips and the escalating risk pill checked at 1280 and 900 px.

## What's left / known issues
- A claim mid-turn with no pause after it can still take ~2 s (the "sure thing" guarantee: 2.26 s).
- Guarantees and fund costs are "Couldn't verify" by design; pressure tactics (M4) will flag guarantees as a tactic.
- The register is a test register for fictional firms; a real regulator lookup is future work.
- The risk score has no tactic signals yet (M4), and weights are hand-set; M4's test set should calibrate them.
- `uvicorn --reload` drops a live call when backend files change (dev only).
