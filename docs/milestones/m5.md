# M5 · Deliver

**Goal (architecture Day 5):** memo and email, UI polish, README with architecture and results, demo video script. **Demo: a full call, end to end.** Target: memo ready ≤ 60 s after hang-up.

## What was built
```
backend/memo/builder.py   the memo, built from facts: at a glance, who called (+ register), what was offered
                          (with "was …"), claims vs. data, pressure (quotes), questions to ask, steps that help;
                          every item with its moment in the call and its source
backend/memo/writer.py    collects the call from the bus; after hang-up, Gemini words the summary (hedged across
                          3.8-flash + flash-lite) under guards (numbers from the facts; no buy/sell/safe/scam/official);
                          otherwise the template stays
backend/memo/store.py     saved calls and memos in SQLite (backend/data/, gitignored)
backend/memo/email.py     Resend: plain HTML + text, Idempotency-Key, 10 s timeout, plain-words fallback
app/main.py               GET /api/calls, GET /api/calls/{id}/memo, GET /api/email, POST /api/calls/{id}/email
frontend                  memo screen (polling, sections, "at m:ss" → transcript, email, print);
                          polish: risk-pill reasons popover, headings, 900 px overflow, "—" explained
eval/run_eval.py          + memo targets (time to memo on real calls, wording check)
README.md, docs/demo-script.md, screenshots in docs/img/
```

## What real runs caught in M5
- `gemini-3.8-flash` returned **400** on the first memo. That was my configuration (it rejects `thinking_level=minimal`), not the outage I'd assumed; the client now picks the thinking level per model.
- Gemini's summary called the test register **"official"**. Now rejected by the word guard, and the "steps that help" text that put the word into the facts was reworded.
- The new memo check flagged Tenor's own reply "What other people **buy**…", now "…**do**…". Quotes of the caller stay exempt.

## Known issues / what's left
- The memo's p95 is 9.1 s: when every Gemini model is slow, the summary waits up to its 20 s budget before the template is used. The memo is still well within target, and the call screen never waits.
- Resend's sandbox sender only delivers to the Resend account's own address. Sending to anyone else needs a verified domain (`MEMO_EMAIL_FROM`).
- Everything in M4's limitations still applies (synthetic benchmark, test register, live mic tested by hand).
