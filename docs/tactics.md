# Pressure tactics

Generated from `backend/detector/taxonomy.py` (the single source for the classifier, the rules, the UI labels and the data briefs). The tactics paraphrase the warning signs that financial regulators publish for investment scams; no text is copied.

| Tactic | Shown to Eszter | What it is | Not this (honest look-alike) | Tenor suggests saying |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `urgency` | Rushing you | Pushes a decision now: a deadline today or within hours, a price 'locked' until a time, 'decide before we hang up'. | A real, distant deadline stated calmly with no push (e.g. a tax-year date, 'no rush'). | “I don't make decisions on the same day. Can you email me the details first?” |
| `scarcity` | “Rare chance” | Says the opportunity is rare, exclusive or limited: only a few places, a select group of clients, 'not offered to the public'. | Plain product facts such as 'this account has no minimum'. | “If it's a good investment, it will still be there next week.” |
| `social_proof` | “Everyone's doing it” | Uses what other people supposedly do or buy to push the listener: 'most of my clients take 10,000', 'thousands have already joined'. | Neutral information without a push, e.g. 'some customers start with a monthly amount, then adjust'. | “What other people do doesn't matter to me. I decide for myself.” |
| `secrecy` | Keeping it secret | Asks the listener not to tell or consult family, friends, the bank or an adviser, or to keep the deal quiet. | Normal privacy, e.g. 'I can't discuss your account with anyone else'. | “I always talk to my family about money. I'll call you back after that.” |
| `guarantee` | Promises no risk | Promises returns or says there is no risk: 'guaranteed', 'risk-free', 'can't lose', 'a sure thing', 'it will double'. | A legal deposit protection stated factually (e.g. 'deposits are insured up to the legal limit'), or 'past returns don't guarantee future results'. | “No one can guarantee a share price. Can you put that promise in writing?” |
| `fomo_regret` | Fear of missing out | Plays on regret or missing out: 'people who waited regretted it', 'don't miss out', 'you'll kick yourself'. | Neutral history without pressure, e.g. 'markets have gone up and down over time'. | “I'm comfortable missing a chance rather than rushing.” |
| `skip_checks` | Skipping the checks | Discourages paperwork, written details or verification: 'no time for paperwork', 'I'll confirm by phone', 'you don't need to read it'. | Offering to send documents or saying 'take your time to read it'. | “I'll only go ahead once I have everything in writing.” |
| `authority` | Borrowed authority | Leans on vague experts, insiders or famous names to persuade: 'our analysts say', 'insiders know', 'it's the next Apple', 'endorsed by …'. | Naming a specific, checkable source of a fact (e.g. 'the fund's factsheet lists the fee'). | “Which document can I read that shows this?” |

**Detection:** a sentence is flagged when the classifier (all-MiniLM-L6-v2 embeddings + one logistic regression per tactic, thresholds tuned for precision on training data) or a high-precision rule (`backend/detector/rules.py`) fires. **Strong** = a rule matched or the classifier is ≥ 0.9 confident; **Mild** = classifier only. Strong adds 15 risk points, mild 3, each tactic counted once.
