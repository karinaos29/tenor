# Manipulative call (M6). Fictional firm (Ashcombe Wealth, not in the register). All names are invented.
# A "security team" asks for the one-time code just texted to Eszter, and she starts reading it out; then the caller asks for her password.
# Format: one turn per line, "CALLER:" or "YOU:". Lines starting with # are ignored.
YOU: Hello?
CALLER: Hello, is this Eszter? This is Mark from the security team at Ashcombe Wealth, where your investment account is held.
YOU: Ashcombe? I'm not sure I have an account there.
CALLER: Someone is trying to withdraw twelve thousand dollars from your account right now. We need to stop it in the next few minutes.
YOU: Oh dear. What should I do?
CALLER: We've just sent a six digit code to your phone. Can you read it to me so I can block the withdrawal?
YOU: Let me look. It says four, seven, two, nine.
CALLER: Good, and the last two numbers?
YOU: Wait. Why do you need my code?
CALLER: It's only to cancel the transfer. Please don't hang up, and don't call your bank, they'll only slow things down.
YOU: I'd rather call my bank myself.
CALLER: Then I'll also need your online banking password, so we can move your savings to a protected account today.
YOU: No. I'm going to hang up now.
